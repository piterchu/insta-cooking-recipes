# Salmon and Avocado Marinated Rice Bowl

⏱️ **40 min** (mostly marinating) | 🍚 **1 portion** | 🟢 **Débutant** | 🐟 **Poisson cru**

## Ingredients

### Base
- 300g sushi-grade salmon
- 1–2 avocados
- Cooked rice, as much as you like

### Marinade
- 100ml soy sauce
- 100ml water
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- Plenty of toasted sesame seeds
- Plenty of chives

### Optional Topping
- 1 egg yolk

## Steps

1. Add all the marinade ingredients to a container and mix well.
2. Slice the salmon into thin strips and cut the avocado into cubes. Add them to the marinade and leave in the fridge for about 30 minutes.
3. Serve over cooked rice and top with an egg yolk if you like.

## Notes personnelles

A simple and fresh poke-style bowl with Japanese-inspired flavors. Perfect for a quick weeknight dinner with minimal cooking.
