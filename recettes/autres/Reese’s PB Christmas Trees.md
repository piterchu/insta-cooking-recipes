### Ingredients

  * 3/4 cup natural peanut butter 
  * 2/3 cup almond flour 
  * 1/4 cup pure maple syrup 
  * 1/2 cup chocolate chips 
  * 1 teaspoon coconut oil 

### Steps

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine the peanut butter, almond flour, and maple syrup. Stir until smooth and dough-like. 
  2. Press out the “dough” to your desired thickness onto parchment paper. Then place it in the freezer for 5–10 minutes to firm up. 
  3. In a small saucepan over low heat, melt the chocolate and coconut oil together, stirring constantly until smooth. 
  4. Remove “dough” from the freezer. Using a tree cookie cutter, cut out trees. Dip each peanut butter tree into the melted chocolate using a fork or spoon. Let any excess drip off, then place them on a parchment-lined tray or plate. 
  5. Transfer the chocolate-coated trees to the freezer and let them chill until fully set (about 15–20 minutes). 
  6. Store extras in the fridge or freezer.
