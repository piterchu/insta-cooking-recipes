### Ingredients

  * 2 large seedless cucumbers (shaved into ribbons or thinly sliced) or 4–6 mini seedless cucumbers 
  * 1/2 small red onion, sliced 
  * 1–2 avocados, cut into small cubes 
  * 1/2 cup toasted walnuts, chopped 
  * 1/2 cup pomegranate seeds 
  * 1/4 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped 
  * Zest + juice of 1 lemon 
  * 2–3 tbsp olive oil 
  * Salt & black pepper, to taste 
  * 1–2 teaspoons sumac

### Steps

  1. Thinly shave or slice the cucumbers and place them in a large bowl. 
  2. Add the sliced red onion and cubed avocado. 
  3. Add chopped walnuts and pomegranate seeds. 
  4. Stir in the fresh parsley. 
  5. Add the lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, pepper, and 1 teaspoon sumac directly over the salad and gently toss until everything is evenly coated. 
  6. Serve immediately for best freshness.
