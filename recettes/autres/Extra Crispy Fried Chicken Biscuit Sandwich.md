# Extra Crispy Fried Chicken Biscuit Sandwich

🍗 Fried Chicken | ⏱️ 25 min | 🍽️ 1 portion | 👨‍🍳 Intermédiaire

## Ingredients

- Fried chicken
- Biscuit
- Secret seasoning
- Crispy batter

## Steps

1. Prepare the chicken with the secret seasoning.
2. Dip the chicken into the crispy batter.
3. Fry the chicken until extra crispy and golden brown.
4. Slice the biscuit and assemble the sandwich with the fried chicken.
5. Serve immediately.
