# Garlic Skins No-Waste Seasonings

## Informations rapides

- Cuisine : fusion
- Type : sauce / assaisonnement
- Temps de préparation : 10 min
- Temps de cuisson : 60 min
- Portions : 4
- Difficulté : débutant
- Protéine principale : végétarien

## Ingrédients

- Peaux de plusieurs têtes d'ail (skins from multiple heads of garlic)

## Étapes

1. Collect the papery skins from several heads of garlic.
2. Dry the garlic skins completely, either in the sun, a dehydrator, or a low oven.
3. Once dry, blend or crush the skins to create three different seasonings: garlic salt, umami seasoning, and smoky barbecue rub.
4. Store the seasonings in airtight containers for pantry use.
