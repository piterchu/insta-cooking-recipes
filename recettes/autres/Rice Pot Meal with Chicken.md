### Ingredients

  * 2 cups of rice 
  * 500g chicken thighs, diced 
  * 1 onion, chopped 
  * 2 cloves garlic, minced 
  * 1 tbsp ginger, grated 
  * 2 tbsp soy sauce 
  * 1 tbsp sesame oil 
  * 3 cups chicken broth 
  * 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, peas, corn) 
  * Salt and pepper to taste 
  * Green onions for garnish

### Steps

  1. Rinse the rice thoroughly and set aside. 
  2. Heat sesame oil in a pot and sauté the chopped onion, garlic, and grated ginger until fragrant. 
  3. Add the diced chicken and cook until lightly golden on all sides. 
  4. Stir in soy sauce, salt, and pepper to season. 
  5. Add the rice to the pot and mix well with the chicken and aromatics. 
  6. Pour in the chicken broth and bring to a boil. 
  7. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15–20 minutes until the rice is cooked and liquid absorbed. 
  8. In the last 5 minutes, add the mixed vegetables and stir gently. 
  9. Turn off the heat and let the pot rest covered for 5 minutes. 
  10. Fluff the rice, garnish with chopped green onions, and serve hot.
