### Ingredients

  * Whole chicken 
  * Soy sauce 
  * Dark soy sauce 
  * Shaoxing wine 
  * Brown sugar 
  * Salt 
  * Water 
  * Aromatics 
  * Chicken skin (keep intact) 

### Steps

  1. Pat the whole chicken dry. 
  2. Prepare a soy-based braising liquid with soy sauce, dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, brown sugar, salt, water, and aromatics. 
  3. Bring the braising liquid to a gentle simmer. 
  4. Submerge the chicken and braise slowly, basting periodically to maintain a glossy finish. 
  5. Cook until the chicken is tender and infused with the savory, aromatic flavors. 
  6. Serve whole as a centerpiece for Lunar New Year, symbolizing unity.
