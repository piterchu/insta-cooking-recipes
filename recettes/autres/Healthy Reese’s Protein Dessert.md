### Ingredients

  * 200 g Greek yogurt 
  * 1 tbsp peanut butter 
  * A small handful of dark chocolate chips 
  * Optional topping: crushed nuts or cocoa powder 

### Steps

  1. Blend the Greek yogurt and peanut butter until smooth. 
  2. Melt the dark chocolate chips and pour them on top. 
  3. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
