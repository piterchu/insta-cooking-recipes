# Healthy and Delicious Steamed Asian Cabbage Rolls

## Informations rapides

🥬 Steamed cabbage rolls | 🍤 Shrimp filling | ⏱️ 35 minutes | 👥 2 portions

Healthy Asian-inspired cabbage rolls filled with savory shrimp, steamed to perfection for a light yet satisfying meal.

## Ingredients

* Cabbage leaves
* Shrimp filling

## Steps

1. Prepare cabbage leaves for rolling.
2. Make a juicy shrimp filling that tastes like dumplings but without the guilt.
3. Roll the shrimp filling inside the cabbage leaves.
4. Steam the cabbage rolls until fully cooked.
5. Serve and enjoy!
