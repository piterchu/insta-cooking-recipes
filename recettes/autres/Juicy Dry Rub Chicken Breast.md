# Juicy Dry Rub Chicken Breast

## Informations rapides
⏱️ 40 min total | 🍗 Poulet | 👥 2 portions | 📊 Débutant | 🔥 Four

## Ingrédients

- 2 large chicken breasts
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt

## Étapes

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
2. Pat the chicken breasts dry with paper towels.
3. Rub each chicken breast lightly with olive oil.
4. In a small bowl, mix all spices together to create the dry rub.
5. Coat the chicken breasts evenly with the dry rub on all sides.
6. Place the chicken breasts on a baking tray lined with parchment paper.
7. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until the internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F).
8. Remove from the oven and allow the chicken to rest for 5 minutes before slicing and serving.
