### Ingredients

  * Whole chicken 
  * Ginger 
  * Scallions 
  * Soy sauce 
  * Shaoxing wine 
  * Cinnamon 
  * And more 

### Steps

  1. Prepare your whole chicken and clean it thoroughly. 
  2. Slice ginger and cut scallions into sections. 
  3. In a pot, add soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, ginger, scallions, cinnamon, and additional desired seasonings. 
  4. Place the whole chicken into the pot and cook for 30 minutes until fully cooked and infused with flavor. 
  5. Serve hot and enjoy this fast, flavorful, and festival-ready dish for Lunar New Year.
