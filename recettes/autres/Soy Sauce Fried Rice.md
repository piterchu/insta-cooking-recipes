# Soy Sauce Fried Rice

📱 Instagram | ⏱️ 20 min | 🍚 4 portions | 🟢 Débutant

## Ingredients

- Cooked rice (preferably day-old)
- Soy sauce
- Garlic, minced
- Eggs
- Green onions, chopped
- Cooking oil
- Optional: mixed vegetables or leftover meats

## Steps

1. Heat oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat.
2. Add minced garlic and sauté until fragrant.
3. Push the garlic to the side and scramble the eggs in the same pan.
4. Add the cooked rice and stir-fry, breaking up any clumps.
5. Pour soy sauce over the rice and mix well.
6. Add chopped green onions and any optional vegetables or meats.
7. Stir-fry for a few more minutes until everything is heated through and evenly coated.
8. Serve hot.
