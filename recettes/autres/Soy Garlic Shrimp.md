# Soy Garlic Shrimp

⏱️ 25 min | 🍤 Fruits de mer | 🌶️ Sucré-salé | 👤 1 portion

## Ingredients

### Main Dish
- 250g Shrimp (raw weight)
- 1 Tsp Soy Sauce
- 2 Tsp Minced Garlic
- 10g Honey
- 2 Tbsp Stevia
- 1 Tsp Sesame Oil
- 1 Tsp Black Pepper
- 2 Tsp Salt
- 1 Tsp Garlic Powder
- ½ Tbsp Corn Starch

### Cucumber Salad
- 1 Cucumber
- 1 Tsp Brown Stevia
- 1 Tsp Garlic Powder or Minced Garlic
- 5ml Sesame Oil
- 5ml Soy Sauce
- Pinch of Gochugaru or Chili Powder
- Salt and Pepper

### Toppings
- 125g Rice (per serving)
- Green Onion
- Sesame Seeds

## Steps

1. Into a bowl add: 250g raw shrimp, 1 Tsp salt, 1 Tsp garlic powder, 1 Tsp black pepper and mix.
2. On medium-high heat, spray pan with cooking oil and cook shrimp until it changes color, then remove from pan.
3. Add to the pan: 1 Tsp soy sauce, 10g honey, 2 Tbsp stevia, 1 Tsp sesame oil.
4. Turn heat down to medium-low, add 25ml of water and allow to simmer.
5. Create corn starch slurry by combining ½ Tbsp water with ½ Tbsp corn starch.
6. Add corn starch slurry to pan and mix until sauce thickens. Remove from heat.
7. In a container combine: sliced cucumber, 1 Tsp minced garlic powder, 1 Tsp brown stevia, 5ml sesame oil, 5ml soy sauce, pinch of gochugaru or chili powder, salt and pepper.
8. Close and shake until evenly coated.
9. Serve with 125g rice, cucumber salad on the side, topped with sesame seeds and green onions. Enjoy!

## Notes personnelles

These measurements are for 1 serving. To make more portions, adjust measurements proportionally for batch cooking/meal prep.
