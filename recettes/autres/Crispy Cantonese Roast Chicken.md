### Ingredients

  * Whole chicken (under 3 lb if using an air fryer)
  * Boiling water
  * Vinegar

### Steps

  1. Cook until the internal temperature hits 165°F. For air fryer cooking, stick to chickens under 3 lb for the best results. 
  2. A quick splash of boiling water tightens the skin instantly, helping it dry faster and crisp up beautifully when cooked. 
  3. Brush the skin with a little vinegar — it helps break down collagen so the skin turns crispy instead of rubbery. 
  4. Start breast-down if you want extra moisture in the white meat. 
  5. If the skin browns too quickly, loosely tent with foil and remove it for the final 5 minutes to finish crisping.
