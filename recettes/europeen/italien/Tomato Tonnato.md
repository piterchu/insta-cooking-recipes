# Tomato Tonnato

## Informations rapides

- Cuisine : italienne
- Type : entrée
- Temps de préparation : 20 min
- Temps de cuisson : 0 min
- Portions : 4
- Difficulté : intermédiaire
- Protéine principale : poisson (thon)

## Ingrédients

**Pickled shallots**
- 1 shallot
- 60ml cider vinegar
- 60ml water
- 1 tsp sugar
- Pinch of salt

**Tonnato sauce**
- 120g homemade Mayo
- 80g tuna in olive oil, drained
- 3 anchovy fillets
- 1 tbsp capers
- Juice of ½ lemon

**Tomatoes**
- 3 large ripe tomatoes

**To finish**
- Fried capers
- Chervil
- Pickled shallots
- Pine nuts
- Shaved Parmesan
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Salt
- Black pepper

## Étapes

1. Prepare the pickled shallots by combining the cider vinegar, water, sugar, and a pinch of salt. Add the shallot and let it pickle.
2. Make the tonnato sauce by blending the mayonnaise, tuna, anchovies, capers, and lemon juice until smooth.
3. Slice the large ripe tomatoes.
4. Arrange the tomatoes on a plate and top with the tonnato sauce.
5. Garnish with fried capers, chervil, pickled shallots, pine nuts, and shaved Parmesan.
6. Drizzle with extra virgin olive oil and season with salt and black pepper before serving.
