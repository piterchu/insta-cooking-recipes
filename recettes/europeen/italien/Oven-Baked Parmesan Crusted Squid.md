# Calamars Panés au Four

## Informations rapides

- Cuisine : italienne
- Type : plat-principal
- Temps de préparation : 15 min
- Temps de cuisson : 25 min
- Portions : 4
- Difficulté : débutant
- Protéine principale : fruits-de-mer

## Ingrédients

- 800g of squid
- 40g (1/2 cup) of parmesan
- Parsley
- 1 Lemon (zest)
- 1 clove of garlic
- 160g (1 1/4 cups) of breadcrumbs
- Salt
- Extra virgin olive oil

## Étapes

1. Slice the squid and transfer it to the bowl. Add the parmesan, parsley, lemon (zest), garlic, breadcrumbs, salt, and olive oil and mix all well.
2. Pour the mixture into the baking pan covered with parchment paper and sprinkle with more olive oil.
3. Transfer the pan to the oven and bake at 190°C/375°F for 25 minutes.
4. Serve on a serving dish and enjoy.
