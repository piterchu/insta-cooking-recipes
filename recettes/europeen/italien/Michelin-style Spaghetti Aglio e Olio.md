# Michelin-style Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

## Informations rapides
⏱️ 25 min | 👥 2 portions | 🔧 Intermédiaire | 🌱 Végétarien

## Ingredients

- Spaghetti
- Garlic
- Olive oil
- Pasta water
- Starch

## Steps

1. Blanch the garlic repeatedly to remove harshness and bitterness, creating a softer and more rounded flavor profile.
2. Blend the blanched garlic into a silky emulsion with olive oil and pasta water.
3. Toss the cooked spaghetti in the emulsion.
4. Serve with a creamy texture – no cream and no butter, just starch, olive oil, garlic, and control.
