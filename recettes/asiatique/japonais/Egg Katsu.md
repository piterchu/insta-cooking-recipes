# Egg Katsu

## Informations rapides

- Cuisine : japonaise
- Type : plat-principal
- Temps de préparation : 15 min
- Temps de cuisson : 10 min
- Portions : 4
- Difficulté : intermédiaire
- Protéine principale : végétarien

## Ingrédients

- Eggs
- Panko breadcrumbs
- Flour
- Oil for frying

## Étapes

1. Prepare three bowls: one with flour, one with beaten eggs, and one with panko breadcrumbs.
2. Boil the eggs until the whites are set but the yolks are still runny, then peel carefully.
3. Dredge each egg in flour, then dip in beaten egg, and coat with panko breadcrumbs.
4. Heat oil in a pan to medium-high.
5. Fry the breaded eggs for about 2 minutes until golden and crispy.
6. Remove and drain on paper towels. Serve hot in sandwiches, burgers, or as desired.
