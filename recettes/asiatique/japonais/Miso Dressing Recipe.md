# Miso Dressing

## Informations rapides

- Cuisine : japonaise
- Type : sauce
- Temps de préparation : 5 min
- Temps de cuisson : 0 min
- Portions : 4
- Difficulté : débutant
- Protéine principale : végétarien

## Ingrédients

- 15g miso (mixed or white)
- 30g onion
- 8g sugar
- 10g rice vinegar
- 2g lemon juice
- 30g rice bran oil

## Étapes

1. Combine all the ingredients and blend until smooth.
2. Store any leftovers in the fridge.
3. The raw onion has a slight bite on the first day, but the flavour becomes much milder the next day!

## Notes personnelles

You can swap the vinegar and oil for other mild-flavoured varieties if you prefer.
