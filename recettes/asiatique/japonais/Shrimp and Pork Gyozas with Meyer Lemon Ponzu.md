# Shrimp and Pork Gyozas with Meyer Lemon Ponzu

## Informations rapides

- Cuisine : japonaise
- Type : entrée
- Temps de préparation : 40 min
- Temps de cuisson : 15 min
- Portions : 4
- Difficulté : intermédiaire
- Protéine principale : fruits-de-mer

## Ingrédients

**Pour la farce :**
- 2 pounds of shrimp
- 1/2 pound ground pork
- 1 medium/small cabbage about 1.5 pounds
- 1 bunch scallions
- 5 garlic cloves
- Thumbsize ginger
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil

**Pour le ponzu :**
- 100 ml soy sauce
- 100 ml meyer lemon juice
- 1.5 Tbsp mirin
- 5 gr bonito flakes
- 1 piece of kombu

## Étapes

1. Start by finely chopping the cabbage, place it in a bowl, and add 2 tsp of salt. Mix well and reserve for at least 20 minutes.
2. Finely chop the shrimp and the rest of the filling ingredients. Place them in a bowl and mix very well.
3. When the cabbage is ready, squeeze it very hard to release all of its liquid.
4. Mix the squeezed cabbage with the rest of the filling ingredients.
5. Make a small patty from the mixture, cook it, and taste it. Adjust the flavor to your preference.
6. For the ponzu, mix all the ingredients and reserve in the fridge. You can do this overnight.
7. Strain the ponzu before serving. If not using the next day, strain it and keep it in the fridge.
