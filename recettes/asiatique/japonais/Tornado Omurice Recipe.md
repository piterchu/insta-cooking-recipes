# Tornado Omurice Recipe

## Informations rapides

⏱️ Préparation: 15 min | 🔥 Cuisson: 20 min | 👥 Portions: 2 | 🎯 Difficulté: Intermédiaire

Omelette japonaise spectaculaire en forme de tornado, servie sur riz frit et sauce demi-glace.

## Ingrédients

### Riz frit
* 4 garlic cloves
* 1/2 onion
* Unsalted butter
* 1 cup white rice
* Ketchup
* Salt
* Black pepper
* 3 eggs
* 1 tbsp milk

### Sauce Demi-glacé
* 1 tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tbsp sugar
* 1 tbsp milk
* 2 tbsp oyster sauce
* 3 tbsp ketchup
* 5 tbsp red wine

## Étapes

1. Chop the garlic and onion.
2. Sauté them in butter until fragrant.
3. Add the rice, a splash of ketchup, salt, and pepper; stir-fry well.
4. Simmer the demi-glacé sauce until slightly thickened.
5. Whisk the eggs with a little milk until smooth.
6. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low heat and add butter or oil.
7. Pour in the egg mixture and cook for 10–15 seconds, until the bottom sets while the top remains slightly runny.
8. Use two chopsticks to gently twist the eggs into the signature tornado shape.
9. Turn off the heat while the egg is still soft and moist.
10. Carefully slide the omelette onto the prepared rice and drizzle with demi-glacé sauce.
11. Serve immediately.
