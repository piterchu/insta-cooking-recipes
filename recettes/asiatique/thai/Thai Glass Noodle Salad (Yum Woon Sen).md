# Thai Glass Noodle Salad (Yum Woon Sen)

## Informations rapides

- Cuisine : thaï
- Type : entrée / salade
- Temps de préparation : 20 min
- Temps de cuisson : 10 min
- Portions : 4
- Difficulté : débutant
- Protéine principale : porc (avec crevettes)

## Ingrédients

- Glass noodles
- Fresh herbs
- Veggies
- Minced pork
- Shrimp
- Lime juice
- Fish sauce
- Chillies
- Sugar

## Étapes

1. Cook the glass noodles according to package instructions and set aside.
2. Prepare fresh herbs and veggies by washing and chopping them as needed.
3. Cook the minced pork until fully done, then set aside.
4. Cook the shrimp until pink and cooked through.
5. Combine the lime juice, fish sauce, chillies, and sugar to create the dressing.
6. Toss the glass noodles, herbs, veggies, pork, and shrimp together in a large bowl.
7. Pour the dressing over the salad and mix thoroughly.
8. Serve immediately and enjoy your Yum Woon Sen.
