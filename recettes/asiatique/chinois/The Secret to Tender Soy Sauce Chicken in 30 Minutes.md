# The Secret to Tender Soy Sauce Chicken in 30 Minutes

## Informations rapides

- Cuisine : chinoise
- Type : plat-principal
- Temps de préparation : 5 min
- Temps de cuisson : 30 min
- Portions : 4
- Difficulté : intermédiaire
- Protéine principale : poulet

## Ingrédients

- Whole chicken
- Soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Shaoxing wine
- Ginger
- Scallions
- Star anise
- And more

## Étapes

1. Prepare a whole chicken and clean it thoroughly.
2. In a pot, combine soy sauce, dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, ginger slices, scallions, and star anise.
3. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.
4. Add the chicken to the pot, making sure it's mostly submerged in the liquid.
5. Simmer and occasionally spoon the sauce over the chicken for 30 minutes until tender.
6. Remove the chicken, slice, and serve with the cooking sauce.
