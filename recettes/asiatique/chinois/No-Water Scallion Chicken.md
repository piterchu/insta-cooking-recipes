# No-Water Scallion Chicken

## Informations rapides

- Cuisine : chinoise
- Type : plat principal
- Temps de préparation : 10 min (+ 1h de marinade minimum)
- Temps de cuisson : 60-90 min
- Portions : 4
- Difficulté : intermédiaire
- Protéine principale : poulet

## Ingrédients

- 1 whole chicken (about 2.2 lb / 1 kg)
- 1 onion, cut into chunks
- 6–7 green onions, cut into sections
- 1 tbsp oil

**Marinade :**
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce

## Étapes

1. Pat the chicken dry. Mix with all marinade ingredients and marinate for at least 1 hour (overnight is even better).
2. Place onion chunks and green onions at the bottom of a Dutch oven. Add the chicken and remaining sauce.
3. Cook over low-medium heat for 1–1.5 hours.
4. Top with shredded scallions and drizzle hot oil before serving.

## Notes personnelles

Save every drop of that sauce — it is AMAZING over rice.

This recipe also works in:
- Rice cooker: Cook one cycle (~60 min), then rest for 30 min.
- Instant Pot: High pressure 25–30 min, natural release 10–15 min.
